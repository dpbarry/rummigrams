Toolbar Controls
Selection mode - tap tiles to add to group
Scatter - send all tiles to the board
Shuffle - randomize tiles in hand
Sort flushes - group tiles by matching numbers
Sort straights - arrange tiles by sequential order
Quick Actions

Right-click a tile (or double-tap on touch) to open a context menu with actions for that tile.

Add to group — marks a tile as part of a selection. Right-click a grouped tile to move or transform the whole set.
Rotate & flip — when two or more board tiles are grouped, right-click one to rotate or mirror the entire arrangement. Greyed options mean there isn't enough space.
